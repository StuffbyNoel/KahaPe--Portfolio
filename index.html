THE CONCEPT

Kaha Pe? ("Where are you?") is an Indian-themed split-screen couch co-op game inspired by the vibrant chaos surrounding a bustling Indian train station. Dropped into separate corners of a crowded neighborhood, players must communicate landmarks, push through dense street crowds to find their friend, and race to the station before the timer runs out and the train departs.

My focus on the project was architecting the procedural MapGenerator in Unreal Engine Blueprints—dynamically generating branching street grids, road junctions, and building plots so every run feels fresh—alongside modeling the modular 3D Indian street buildings that bring the neighborhood to life.
